

TF_LOG → controls Terraform logging level, mainly for debugging errors.


Example: Terraform gives an error

You run:

terraform plan

and get:

Error: Failed to load provider



You can enable detailed logs:

export TF_LOG=DEBUG
terraform plan

Now Terraform prints extra technical information about what it is doing.






Problem
   ↓
TF_LOG=DEBUG
   ↓
terraform plan
